My approach

Useful automation, not automation for its own sake.

I aim for workflows that are understandable, testable, and designed with failure cases in mind.

✓Clear process mapping
Define the trigger, expected inputs, and success criteria.
✓Reliable integrations
Validate payloads, credentials, retries, and error paths.
✓Human oversight
Use review steps where decisions or external actions need approval.
✓Documented handoff
Provide setup notes, workflow screenshots, and test evidence.
